# Shopping flow fixes — 2026-11-05

The existing application now keeps the customer's chosen branch through quantity edits and reloads, and keeps unfinished list drafts separate from the priced cart. No synthetic prices, inventory claims, retailer checkout integration, or paid AI calls were introduced.

## Implemented

- Manual branch preference is scoped to city/branch filters and persisted. Automatic cheapest-store selection remains available explicitly.
- Pending list lines no longer suppress a valid full-cart price. The draft has its own continue/delete controls.
- List selections and edited quantities persist. Restored products are revalidated against the catalog before adding. One row is open at a time, with three initial choices and an optional expansion.
- Catalog and in-cart search use the same add/remove quantity controls. In-cart search displays actual local unit prices, separate from the chosen branch's cart total.
- Previous quotes remain explicitly labelled during a refresh. Changed quantities and alternatives cannot inherit an old line subtotal. Quotes from another geographic scope are not retained.
- Equal-price branches within a chain are grouped, with individual branch details and selection retained. Partial and stale baskets stay outside full-price ranking.
- Back closes dialogs before leaving a view; per-view scroll and the category position are retained.
- Reduced mobile homepage/summary/card height, one scroll surface in quick search, larger controls and typography, smaller floating cart strip.
- Alternative cards show purchase quantity and known total pack volume/overage. Advanced differences and quantity editing are collapsed together. Different manufacturer metadata is described as a source-field difference, not an asserted manufacturer change.
- In meal planning, a candidate button explicitly states the known purchase amount. Selecting it advances to the next ingredient; uncertain packaging still needs manual quantity confirmation. Final transfer to the cart remains explicit.
- Singular purchase labels corrected across touched shopping flows; equivalent unit spellings no longer generate redundant replacement sentences.
- Automatic recalculations are coalesced into shopping sessions instead of incrementing history for every quantity change.
- Retailer handoff is labelled as copying a list and opening a retailer website. Unsupported selected chains receive a specific explanation.
- Corrected `rami-levy--64` from Haifa to Or Akiva using the official branch directory. Original city and verification source/date remain recorded. The correction requires the exact chain, branch ID, branch name and address; it does not infer other branch locations.

## Validation

- 137 automated Node tests passed; build and JS syntax checks passed.
- Real official snapshot arithmetic/provenance test made deterministic at the snapshots' publication time, so advancing wall-clock time does not make arithmetic assertions depend on freshness. Separate tests still reject stale prices.
- Browser QA at 390 × 844 viewport (375 CSS content pixels with scrollbar) and desktop 1348 CSS pixels. This is browser viewport testing, not a physical-phone test.
- Verified: search/add/remove; quantity repricing; chosen branch preserved through edits and reload; price retained with ten pending draft lines; close/reopen draft selection; browser Back closes modal; bulk transfer leaves unresolved lines in the draft; local prices in quick search; automatic cheapest-store reset; dark mode; meal template candidate selection advances after explicit displayed-quantity selection; alternative amount details.
- Actual local snapshot example: two milk units at 7.35 each kept the selected branch and displayed 14.70. Adding bread left that branch's basket partial; explicit automatic selection chose a complete branch with 14.70 + 10.05 = 24.75. Local per-item minima were not incorrectly summed as a single-store total.
- Ten-line draft rendered one open row and three candidate buttons, versus the audited 80 choices. Its rows occupied 1,552 px versus 7,655 px in the audited version. An inspected alternative card measured 296 px versus approximately 405 px before.
- No application-origin browser console error observed in tested flows; unrelated browser-extension errors were excluded.

## Remaining limits

- Photographs exist only when a verified barcode image is available; category illustrations remain explicitly labelled. No claim of complete image coverage.
- Missing price is not proof of missing stock. Source price freshness and collection health remain visible and can limit the branches ranked.
- Public freeform AI remains disabled; structured meal/recipe planning continues to work without paid inference.
- Direct cart transfer/payment at a retailer is not integrated. The existing list-copy/link handoff is now explicit.
- Camera, microphone, physical mobile devices, and every catalog item were not manually tested in this change.
